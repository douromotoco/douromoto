Cornering Masterclass

The Apex & Counter-Steering

To negotiate curves smoothly, look through the turn to your exit. Apply light pressure on the inside handlebar grip (counter-steering) at higher speeds to lean the machine effortlessly.

Braking Dynamics

70/30 Trail Braking Rule

Approximately 70% of your stopping power comes from the front brake due to weight transfer. Master gradual front lever squeezing combined with rear brake modulation for wet traction stability.

Pre-Ride T-CLOCS Check
