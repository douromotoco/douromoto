GEAR & SAFETY

Certifications, standards, and essential protection.

Safety First

Always Verify Certifications

Never compromise on head and spine protection. Look for ECE 22.06, Snell M2020, and CE EN 17092 ratings.

Helmets & Safety Standards

ECE 22.06, DOT & Snell M2020

The helmet is your most critical gear. Modern standards like ECE 22.06 (mandatory in Europe and globally accepted) test rotational impacts, multi-point drop tests, and visor integrity far more rigorously than older standards. DOT is US standard, while Snell represents extreme motorsport racing requirements.

Riding Jackets & CE Ratings

EN 17092 Standards (Class AAA to C)

Look for CE EN 17092 certification ratings: Class AAA for full leather racing suits, Class AA for touring jackets, and Class A/B for urban gear. Always ensure Level 2 CE armor (EN 1621).
