Riding Gear & Global Safety Standards

Safety first is the DMC moto. Know your certifications before buying helmets, jackets, and armor.

Helmet Standard Global Gold Standard

ECE 22.06 Certification

Replacing the older ECE 22.05 standard, ECE 22.06 introduces rigorous rotational impact testing, oblique crash angles, and strict visor penetration tests. Helmets bearing this certification are mandatory across professional racing leagues and top street riding communities globally.

Key Takeaways:
• Multi-impact rotational testing
• Mandatory integrated sun visor safety evaluations
• Strict shell deformation limits
Apparel Standard CE EN 17092

Protective Jacket & Pants Ratings

CE rating classes range from Class A (light urban protection) to Class AAA (heavy-duty leather racing suits). Always look for CE Level 2 armor inserts at shoulders, elbows, knees, and back for maximum impact absorption during a slide.

Armor Rating Levels:
• **Level 1**: Transmits maximum residual force under 18kN
• **Level 2**: Superior protection, transmitting under 9kN
• **AAA Class**: Heavy racing leather & abrasion-resistant textiles
